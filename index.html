ABOUT ME

Jinfeng Wang

Also known as Jacob

Lecturer · Researcher in Medical AI

Kunming University of Science and Technology

I am Jinfeng Wang (Jacob), a Lecturer at Kunming University of Science and Technology and a Visiting Scholar with the MIRACLE research group at the University of Science and Technology of China. I received my PhD in Mathematical Sciences from the University of Liverpool in 2024, with doctoral research conducted at Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University.

My research interests include medical image analysis and segmentation, self-supervised representation learning, multimodal medical AI, EEG decoding and brain–computer interfaces, medical embodied intelligence, and exoskeletons for rehabilitation.
